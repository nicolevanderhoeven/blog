How do you test an AI agent whose whole job is to investigate incidents — when every investigation is different and "correct" isn't a single deterministic answer? In this interview I sit down with Will to talk about **Investigations Arena**, the setup Grafana built to evaluate Grafana Assistant's investigations. We get into why traditional pass/fail testing breaks down for agentic investigation workflows, how the arena lets us pit approaches against each other and score them, and what it takes to build confidence that Assistant is actually helping rather than confidently wandering off. It's a look under the hood at the unglamorous-but-essential work of making an AI agent trustworthy.
