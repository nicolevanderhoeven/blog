I joined [Malik Alimoekhamedov](https://malikalimoekhamedov.substack.com/) on his podcast [*The Mechanics of Knowledge Management*](https://malikalimoekhamedov.substack.com/) for a long conversation about Obsidian, PKM, and the Renaissance-era *zibaldone* tradition — the personal commonplace books that thinkers like Leonardo da Vinci kept as a hybrid of journal, reference, and idea workshop. We talked about how a modern Obsidian vault can play the same role for someone trying to think across domains, and about what changes when you treat your notes as the *exhaust of learning* rather than the destination.
