It's day one of Grafana Labs' AI Week, and Monday is all about **planning** — getting the clarity you need on what's going on in your system so you can figure out what to build. Kicking things off is **Workspace for Grafana Assistant**: a full-page view of Assistant so your conversations and generated graphs aren't crammed into the little right-hand sidebar. It's a much less cluttered place to run investigations and to be agent-first. On top of that, Assistant is now easier to try — enable it in Grafana 13 for OSS without linking a Grafana Cloud account, or try it with no signup or download at all on [Grafana Play](https://play.grafana.org).
