MCP vs CLI: which one should your AI agent actually use? In this episode of *The Context Window* — Grafana's livestream series on AI in observability — we get into the two main ways an AI agent can reach the outside world: the Model Context Protocol (MCP) and good old command-line interfaces. We look at when each one makes sense, the tradeoffs around context, tool sprawl, and reliability, and we walk through Grafana's cloud and open-source MCP servers along with `gcx`, the Grafana command-line experience for agents.
